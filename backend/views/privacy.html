Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Happy Renting ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Happy Renting mobile application and the website at happyrenting.netlify.app (collectively, the "Platform"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Platform.

By using the Platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Platform.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, phone number, and password (hashed and salted with bcrypt).
  • Profile Information: Profile photo, UPI ID, UPI number, bank account details (account holder name, account number, bank name, IFSC code), and QR code images for payment.
  • Tenancy Data: Move-in date, advance paid, security deposit, property address, room number, and tenancy notes.
  • Co-Occupant Information: Names, phone numbers, and ID proof documents of co-occupants you add to your tenancy.
  • Payment Data: Rent payment records, transaction IDs, payment methods, receipts, and payment proof images (screenshots or receipts).
  • Complaint Data: Complaint descriptions, categories, images, and resolution notes related to maintenance issues.
  • ID Proof Documents: Identification documents uploaded during tenancy registration (e.g., Aadhaar, Driver's License, Passport).
  • Communications: Any messages, comments, or inquiries you send us via email at support@happyrenting.in.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and device name (for push notification delivery).
  • Push Notification Tokens: Expo push tokens used to send notifications to your device.
  • IP Address: Your IP address is captured for login security alerts and audit logging.
  • User-Agent: Browser or app version information for security and compatibility purposes.
  • Usage Data: Actions performed within the Platform (e.g., payments, complaints, profile updates) are logged with timestamps.

1.3 Biometric Data

If you enable biometric authentication (Face ID or Fingerprint), your biometric data never leaves your device. We use expo-local-authentication and expo-secure-store to verify your identity locally on your device. No biometric data is transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Tenancy Management: Manage your rental agreements, track payments, and communicate with your property owner.
  • Rent Processing: Generate bills, process online payments via Cashfree, and facilitate manual payment verification with proof images.
  • Notifications: Send push notifications for bill generation, payment confirmation, payment reminders, overdue alerts, and complaint updates.
  • Maintenance Requests: Enable you to file and track complaints and maintenance requests with your property owner.
  • Owner Payouts: Process withdrawal requests to owners' bank accounts or UPI IDs.
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and other security incidents.
  • Legal Compliance: Maintain records as required by applicable laws and regulations.
  • Platform Improvement: Analyze aggregate usage patterns to improve our services (no individual profiling).

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, our legal basis for processing your personal data is:

  • Contractual Necessity: Processing is required to perform our agreement with you (manage tenancy, process payments).
  • Legitimate Interests: For security, fraud prevention, and platform improvement, provided your interests and fundamental rights do not override those interests.
  • Consent: Where you have explicitly consented (e.g., push notification permissions, biometric authentication).
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with applicable laws (e.g., retention of financial records).

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 With Property Owners

Your name, tenancy details, payment history, and complaint information are shared with your property owner (the "Owner") as necessary to manage your tenancy.

4.2 Third-Party Service Providers

  • Cashfree Payments (India): For processing online rent payments. Cashfree is PCI-DSS compliant. Your payment data is handled in accordance with Cashfree's privacy policy.
  • Expo (US): For push notification infrastructure. Device push tokens are relayed through Expo's push notification service.
  • Cloudinary (US): For storing payment proof images and QR code images with private access controls.
  • Resend (US): For transactional email delivery (notifications, login alerts, receipts).
  • MongoDB Atlas (US/EU): For hosting our database. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests by public authorities (e.g., court order, government agency).

4.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Passwords: Hashed and salted using bcrypt with 12 salt rounds.
  • Authentication: JWT tokens with 7-day expiry, transmitted over HTTPS only.
  • API Security: Rate limiting (100 requests/15 min), Helmet security headers, CORS whitelisting, NoSQL injection sanitization, and HTTP parameter pollution prevention.
  • Payment Webhooks: HMAC-SHA256 signature verification for Cashfree payment callbacks.
  • On-Device Storage: Auth tokens and biometric credentials are stored in Expo SecureStore (encrypted, OS-level secure storage) on your mobile device.
  • Web Storage: Session tokens are stored in HTTP-only cookies (SameSite=Lax, Secure flag) and localStorage.
  • Image Storage: Uploaded images are stored on Cloudinary with private access controls and HTTPS delivery.
  • Database: MongoDB Atlas with encryption at rest and TLS for data in transit.
  • Server Logs: Operational logs are retained for 14 days with automatic rotation.

6. Data Retention

  • Personal Data: Retained for the duration of your tenancy and up to 30 days after vacating or account closure.
  • Payment Records: Retained for 7 years as required by Indian tax and financial regulations.
  • Server Logs: Retained for 14 days.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Retained until you disable push notifications or delete your account.

What happens when you delete your account:

The following data is permanently deleted or anonymized within 30 days of your confirmed deletion request:

  • Name, email address, and phone number (anonymized)
  • Profile information and preferences
  • Authentication credentials and session tokens
  • Push notification tokens
  • Uploaded identity documents (PAN, Aadhaar, etc.)
  • Complaint history
  • Notification history

The following data is retained due to legal and financial obligations:

  • Payment transaction records (7 years)
  • Rent ledger records (7 years)
  • Financial audit logs (7 years)
  • Withdrawal history (7 years)
  • Invoice and receipt records (7 years)

This retained data is anonymized so it can no longer be linked to your identity. If you are a tenant, your property owner will be notified of the deletion request.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data we hold.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated personal data via our Data Deletion page. You will receive a confirmation email with a link to verify your request. Processing takes up to 30 days after confirmation. Financial records are retained as required by law (see Section 6).
  • Portability: Request a machine-readable copy of your data.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for push notifications or biometric authentication at any time via app settings.
  • Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Restrict: Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@happyrenting.in. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Platform is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data is processed and stored on servers located in India and the United States. If you are accessing the Platform from outside these countries, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in these locations. We ensure appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place for such transfers.

10. Cookies and Local Storage

The web version of our Platform uses cookies and browser localStorage for authentication session persistence. Specifically:

  • hr_token cookie: Stores your authentication token (30-day expiry, SameSite=Lax, Secure flag).
  • hr_user (localStorage): Caches basic user profile data for UI personalization.

We do not use cookies for advertising, tracking, or analytics purposes. You may clear these at any time through your browser settings.

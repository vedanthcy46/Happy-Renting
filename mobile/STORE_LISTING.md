## Full description (keyword-optimized)

```
Happy Renting is the easiest way for tenants, landlords and property owners to manage rent and property finances — all from one simple app.

WHY HAPPY RENTING?

Paying rent should be effortless. Happy Renting brings your entire rental management workflow onto your phone: view monthly rent bills, pay on time, track due dates, and stay on top of overdue payments. No more missed rent, no more confusing spreadsheets, no more chasing your landlord.

PAY RENT ONLINE, YOUR WAY

• Multiple payment methods – pay rent via UPI, credit/debit card, net banking, cash or cheque, all recorded automatically.
• Monthly rent bills – automatic rent tracking with clear bills generated every month.
• Smart payment status – see at a glance whether rent is Pending, Partially Paid, Paid or Overdue.
• Payment proof upload – snap a photo of your UPI/cash/bank transfer receipt and upload it for instant owner verification.
• Secure payments – processed through trusted gateways (Cashfree); Happy Renting never stores your card or UPI credentials.
• Digital rent receipts – generate and share professional rent receipts whenever you need them.

RAISE & TRACK COMPLAINTS

• Report maintenance issues, repairs, plumbing, electrical or house-related problems in seconds.
• Real-time complaint tracking with status updates and notifications so you always know what's happening.

MANAGE EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE

• Complete rent payment history and transaction log.
• Push notifications for rent due dates, payment confirmations and complaint updates.
• Quick, secure login with biometrics – fingerprint or Face ID.
• Light and dark mode with a modern, easy-to-use interface.
• Built for tenants, landlords, property owners and admins with role-based access control.

Whether you're renting a house, flat, apartment or PG, Happy Renting helps you and your landlord stay organized, transparent and on time.

Download Happy Renting today and make rent management simple, secure and stress-free.
```
